01 Music You Like｜喜欢的音乐

Used to vs. Be used to

used to + 动词原形：过去经常做，现在可能已经改变。I used to listen to pop music.

be used to + 名词 / V-ing：现在已经习惯。I'm used to listening to music every day.

Did you use to like folk music? · I didn't use to like it.

04 Writing About a Song｜歌曲评论

用五个维度组织一篇完整评论

维度实用表达
Voice 声音She can hit some really high notes. / The singer doesn't have much of a range.
Melody / Tune 旋律The tune is catchy. I can't get it out of my head.
Rhythm 节奏You can really dance to it. / There isn't much of a beat.
Lyrics 歌词The lyrics are romantic and meaningful.
Feeling / Mood 情绪It makes me feel relaxed. I like how the mood changes.

Writing Structure

  1. Introduction：I recently listened to...
  2. Description：The melody / voice / lyrics are...
  3. Feeling：It makes me feel...
  4. Opinion：I'd definitely recommend it.

Song Review · My Heart Will Go On

I recently listened to "My Heart Will Go On" by Celine Dion. It's the theme song from the movie Titanic.

The melody is beautiful and sentimental. Celine Dion has an amazing voice, and she can hit some really high notes. The lyrics are very romantic and meaningful.

This song makes me feel relaxed and emotional. It also reminds me of the love story in Titanic. I'd definitely recommend it.

易混词：This song is relaxing（歌曲令人放松）; it makes me feel relaxed（我感到放松）。

05 Speaking Practice｜个性化口语训练

8 道 Lesson 1 模拟中的精选优化答案

音乐喜好与变化

I listen to almost every kind of music, including pop, punk, country, and folk music. I used to listen to pop music because I found it relaxing and soothing, but now I prefer punk or rock because it's more energetic.

听音乐的日常习惯

I usually listen to music while I'm reading because it helps me relax. I also listen to music while exercising because it gives me energy.

音乐与回忆

Movie and TV theme songs remind me of the stories and scenes. For example, "My Heart Will Go On" from Titanic always reminds me of the movie.

音乐与情绪

Relaxing music helps me calm down, while energetic music makes me feel more motivated.

AI 生成音乐

AI-generated music can reduce the cost of music production. However, I don't think it can replace human creativity. AI still has limitations when it comes to creating original music and expressing human emotions.

我的音乐推荐

I'd recommend the soundtracks from Hayao Miyazaki's films, such as Castle in the Sky. I also like the music from Kikujiro's Summer, Taylor Swift's "Love Story," and punk rock—especially "Life Is Hot Because of You" by New Pants.

06 Final Review｜重点复习

把词汇、语法和个人表达连成完整回答

回答音乐话题的顺序

  1. 先说整体喜好和音乐类型。
  2. 补充过去与现在的变化。
  3. 说明收听时间、场景和情绪影响。
  4. 举一首歌或一位音乐人的例子。
  5. 最后给出原因、评价或推荐。
建议背诵：I'm used to listening to music every day. · The tune is catchy. · Music can change my mood. · AI can't replace human creativity. · I'd definitely recommend it.
