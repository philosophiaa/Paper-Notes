Shopping: Bargaining 讨价还价

German PhD Survival English — 德国博士生存英语第一课：学会在购物场景中讨价还价，构建个人回答库。

目标：德国博士生存英语 · 敢开口、能表达、有自己的答案
01 核心词汇 Vocabulary
bargain
a good deal / a transaction — 划算的交易
"It's a bargain." 很划算 / 成交
windfall
unexpected money — 意外之财
"I received a windfall." 获得一笔意外之财
set somebody back
cost somebody a certain amount — 花了某人多少钱
"The phone set me back a lot of money." 这个手机花了我很多钱
make an exception
allow something different from the usual rule — 破例
"Could you make an exception for me?" 可以为我破例吗？

03 个人回答库 Personal Answer Bank

不是背单词，而是准备自己的答案——被问到的时候能直接说出来。

What do you think of bargaining?
Personally, I prefer shopping in malls because the prices are clearly marked. I don't really like bargaining because I sometimes feel uncomfortable doing it. However, if the price is obviously unreasonable, I would try to negotiate. But I wouldn't bargain just to get a small advantage.
Why don't you like bargaining?
Actually, I'm not very good at bargaining because I don't know how to negotiate with sellers.
Should people learn how to bargain?
Not everyone needs to learn how to bargain. It depends on the culture and the situation. However, I think basic communication skills are useful everywhere.
What is the most expensive thing you've ever bought?
The most expensive thing I've ever bought is my iPhone. I didn't bargain because I bought it from a mall where the price was fixed. However, I regretted it a little because I bought some accessory packages, like a phone case, a screen protector, and a power bank. Later, I found out they were much cheaper online.
