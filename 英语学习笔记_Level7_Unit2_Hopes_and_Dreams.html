Embedded Questions｜嵌入式疑问句

疑问词 + 主语 + 谓语，使用陈述句语序。

Where is the law school? → Do you know where the law school is?

What should I do? → I’ve been thinking about what I should do.

常见错误：Do you know where is the school? → Do you know where the school is?

First Conditional｜第一条件句

If + 一般现在时, will + 动词原形.

If she moves abroad, she’ll learn another language.

If you go to law school, you’ll be very successful.

02 Discussing Life Choices｜讨论人生选择

犹豫、假设、遗憾和重要人生决定

Expressing Uncertainty｜表达不确定
I don’t know yet.I haven’t decided yet.I’m not sure.I just can’t decide.I’m undecided.I don’t know what I’m going to do.
Words with -ing｜-ing 的三种用法
用法示例
Adjective 形容词My job is challenging. / The work was rewarding.
Gerund 动名词Working during the day and studying at night isn’t easy.
Progressive 进行时I am studying to become a teacher.

Second Conditional｜第二条件句

If + 一般过去时, would + 动词原形.

If I had a lot of money, I’d travel around the world.

If I lived in Germany, I’d meet people from different countries.

be 动词通常用 were：If I were younger, I’d travel more.

理解重点：第二条件句中的过去时不表示过去，而表示现在或未来不太可能、纯属假设的情况。
The Verb Wish｜愿望与遗憾
结构用途例句
wish + 一般过去时希望现在情况不同I wish I were younger.
wish + could / would希望能力或情况改变I wish I could speak French.
wish + had + 过去分词对过去感到遗憾I wish I had started learning English earlier.

Look Forward To｜表达期待

look forward to + 名词 / V-ing

I’m looking forward to studying in Germany.

I’m looking forward to experiencing a different culture.

注意：这里的 to 是介词，后接 V-ing。不能说 I look forward to study …

04 Personal Speaking Practice｜个人口语练习

围绕机器人研究、德国博士与个人成长的答案库

未来五年的目标

In the next five years, I’d like to improve my English and develop my professional skills.

未来五年，我希望提高英语水平并提升专业技能。

职业兴趣与梦想

I’m interested in robotics, especially robot control and artificial intelligence. I hope to develop smarter robots in the future.

需要提升的能力

I think I need to improve my technical skills, learn more about AI, improve my English, and gain more experience.

面临的困难

The biggest challenge for me is adapting to a new environment, especially the language. That’s why I’m taking this class to improve my English.

去德国读博士

I’ve already quit my job, and I’m currently waiting for my visa to study for a PhD in Germany.

面对挑战

I’m a little worried, but I’m willing to try.

未来学习计划

I will practice speaking every day, listen to English podcasts, read academic papers, and practice with native speakers.

对未来的期待

I’m most looking forward to experiencing a different culture and learning new things with people from different countries. I think it will be an amazing experience.

06 Key Speaking Expressions｜重点表达

按沟通功能快速复习

表达愿望

I hope to … · I’d like to … · My dream is to … · My biggest dream is to …

表达计划

I plan to … · I intend to … · I need to … · I’m working hard to …

表达犹豫与假设

I’m not sure. · I haven’t decided yet. · If I had more time, I would … · If I had the chance, I would …

表达遗憾与期待

I wish I had … · I wish I could … · I wish I were … · I’m looking forward to … · I’m willing to …
