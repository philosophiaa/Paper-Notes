01 Confidential Information｜机密信息

说明信息保密、要求保守秘密及礼貌拒绝

This is confidential information.
这是机密信息。
I'm not supposed to tell anyone.
我不应该／不被允许告诉任何人。
Nobody knows about it except the boss.
除了老板，没有人知道。
I'm not allowed to share the details.
我不被允许透露具体细节。
Asking Someone to Keep a Secret
Don't tell anybody about this.Keep this to yourself, would you?You can't tell anyone, OK?keep a secret
固定搭配：Keep this to yourself. 不要说 Keep it this to yourself；this 已经指代“这件事”。

Responding & Deflecting

A: I'm not supposed to tell anyone. B: OK. Then don't tell me.

A: The information is confidential. B: But you can tell me, right? Come on.

Never mind. It's nothing to worry about.（算了，没什么值得担心的。）

02 Office & Company Issues｜办公室与公司问题

从日常设备故障到数据安全事件

英文表达中文含义
The bathrooms are out of order.洗手间无法使用。
The phone lines are down.电话线路中断。
The company intranet isn't working.公司内部网络无法使用。
The projector is out of action.投影仪暂时无法使用。
Our databases were hacked.数据库遭到黑客攻击。
The company lost millions due to a major product defect.公司因重大产品缺陷损失数百万。
Customer information was stolen.客户信息被盗。
out of order 发生故障be down 中断out of action 暂时停用product defect 产品缺陷intranet 内部网络

03 Passive Voice｜被动语态

关注事件或受影响对象，而非执行者

be + past participle

The data was stolen. · The system was hacked. · The project was delayed.

过去时被动：was / were + 过去分词

现在完成时被动：has / have + been + 过去分词

It hasn't been announced yet.

场景提示：事故汇报中，被动语态适合描述“数据被盗、系统被攻击、问题预计被解决”等结果。

04 Speaking Practice｜口语模拟

机器人公司、机密项目与服务器故障场景

1 · 告知信息机密

I'm sorry, but this is confidential information. I'm not supposed to share it with anyone.

2 · 礼貌拒绝追问

I'm sorry, but I still can't share this information. I hope you understand.

3 · 解释不能透露的原因

It's confidential. It hasn't been announced yet. Only the manager knows about it, and I'm not allowed to share the details.

4 · 描述服务器故障

We're having a technical problem. The server is down, so nobody can access the system.

5 · 汇报严重程度与措施

It's a serious issue. It may affect all the staff in our department. We're investigating the problem and working on a fix.

Common Corrections｜常见纠错

05 Incident Update｜故障通知邮件

预计解决时间、更新承诺与礼貌结尾

Recommended Closing

We expect the issue to be resolved within a few hours. We'll give you an update when we have more information. Thanks for your understanding, and please bear with us as we resolve the issue.

我们预计问题将在几小时内解决。有更多信息时会更新进展。感谢理解，请在问题处理期间耐心等待。

06 Review｜复习路线

按标准流程组织技术问题汇报

Issue → Impact → Action → ETA → Update

  1. 说明问题是什么。
  2. 说明严重程度及影响范围。
  3. 说明正在采取的措施。
  4. 给出预计解决时间。
  5. 承诺后续更新并感谢理解。
建议背诵：This is confidential information. · The server is down, so nobody can access the system. · We're investigating the problem and working on a fix. · We'll give you an update when we have more information.
