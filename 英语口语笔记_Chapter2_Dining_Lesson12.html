01 CORE VOCABULARY 核心词汇

餐厅场景高频词汇，词义 + 例句，直接积累可复用的表达。

free of charge
免费
Is this bread free of charge? 这份面包免费吗？
main course
主菜
I’ll have salmon for my main course. 我要三文鱼作为主菜。
special / today’s specials
特色菜 / 今日特色菜
What specials do you have tonight? 你们今晚有什么特色菜？
offer
提供；供应
What dishes do you offer? 你们供应哪些菜？
appetizer / starter
前菜、开胃菜
We’ll start with the appetizers. 我们先上前菜。
side dish
配菜
I’d like a side dish of vegetables. 我想要一份蔬菜配菜。
dessert
甜点
What dessert do you recommend? 你推荐什么甜点？
menu
菜单
Could I see the menu, please? 可以给我看一下菜单吗？
reservation
预订
I have a reservation for two. 我预订了两个人的位子。
bill (UK) / check (US)
账单
Could we have the bill, please? 可以结账吗？
tip
小费
Should we leave a tip here? 我们要留小费吗？
split the bill
平摊账单
Let’s split the bill. 我们平摊账单吧。

03 MAIN COURSE VS. STAPLE FOOD 主菜 vs 主食

两个容易混淆的概念，点餐时经常一起出现。

概念含义例子
main course 主菜 通常指一餐中最主要的一道菜 牛排、烤鸡或三文鱼
staple food 主食 餐食中作为主体的主食 米饭、面条、馒头

EXAMPLE SENTENCES 例句

I’d like the roast chicken for my main course.
我要烤鸡作为主菜。

Can I have a bowl of rice with it?
可以配一碗米饭吗？

I usually have rice as my staple food.
我通常以米饭为主食。

04 EATING OUT AND FOOD DELIVERY 外出就餐与外卖

聊"平时怎么吃饭"的高频表达：外出、外卖、合租与厨具。

eat out 外出就餐
order food delivery 点外卖（送到家）
order takeout 点外卖 / 外带
cooking equipment 厨具
share an apartment 合租
roommate 室友
not eat much 饭量不大
cook at home 在家做饭

USEFUL EXPRESSIONS 实用句

I often order food delivery.
我经常点外卖。

I usually order food delivery because it’s convenient.
我通常点外卖，因为很方便。

I share an apartment with other people, and I don’t have any cooking equipment.
我跟别人合租，而且没有厨具。

I don’t eat much, so I don’t cook very often.
我饭量不大，所以不经常做饭。

I usually order food or eat something simple.
我通常点外卖或者吃点简单的东西。

I don’t eat out very often.
我不太经常外出就餐。

05 DISCUSSION TOPICS 讨论话题

课堂讨论参考：观点 + 理由 + 细节，用自己的话回答。

How often do you eat out? Who do you usually go with?
IDEAS 参考思路
I don’t eat out very often. I usually order food delivery or eat something simple. I share an apartment with other people, and I don’t have any cooking equipment, so cooking at home is hard for me.
I don’t eat much, so I don’t cook very often either.
Do you like trying new restaurants, or do you prefer familiar ones?
USEFUL PHRASES 实用短语
try different dishes 尝试不同的菜
have a new experience 获得新体验
stick to my favorite restaurants 一直去自己喜欢的餐厅
avoid disappointing experiences 避免令人失望的体验
I like to try different dishes because I enjoy having a new experience. But sometimes I also stick to my favorite restaurants to avoid disappointing experiences.
How can people develop healthy eating habits?
USEFUL PHRASES 实用短语
eat a balanced diet 均衡饮食
eat regular meals 规律吃饭
eat more vegetables and fruit 多吃蔬菜和水果
cut down on processed food 减少加工食品
control portion sizes 控制每餐食量
People can eat a balanced diet and have regular meals. It also helps to eat more vegetables and fruit, cut down on processed food, and control portion sizes.

06 SPEAKING REMINDER 口语提示

准备想法和关键词，而不是完整稿子

上课时尽量用自己的话自然回答。讨论题按照固定结构组织答案，逻辑更清晰、也更容易临场发挥：

Answer直接回答
→
Reason给出理由
→
One detail or example一个细节或例子

例如："I don’t eat out very often. Because I don’t have cooking equipment. For example, I usually order food delivery."
