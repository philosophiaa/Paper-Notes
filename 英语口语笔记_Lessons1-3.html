01 Lesson 1 · Bargaining 讨价还价
LESSON 1

场景：学会在购物中讨价还价，说出自己的立场，并完成个人答案库。

核心词汇 Vocabulary
bargain
a good deal / a transaction — 划算的交易；成交
"It's a bargain." 很划算 / 成交
set somebody back
cost somebody a certain amount — 花了某人多少钱
"The phone set me back a lot of money." 这个手机花了我很多钱
make an exception
allow something different from the usual rule — 破例
"Could you make an exception for me?" 可以为我破例吗？
windfall
unexpected money — 意外之财
"I received a windfall." 获得一笔意外之财
常用表达 Useful Expressions
1
Could you go down on the price?
可以便宜一点吗？
2
That would be my final offer.
这是我的最终报价。
3
The prices are clearly marked.
价格明码标价。
4
I'm not very good at bargaining.
我不太擅长讨价还价。
5
I don't know how to negotiate with sellers.
我不知道怎么和卖家谈价格。
6
It depends on the culture and the situation.
这取决于文化和具体情况。
个人答案库 Personal Answer Bank
What do you think of bargaining?
Personally, I prefer shopping in malls because the prices are clearly marked. I don't really like bargaining because I sometimes feel uncomfortable doing it. However, if the price is obviously unreasonable, I would try to negotiate. But I wouldn't bargain just to get a small advantage.
Why don't you like bargaining?
Actually, I'm not very good at bargaining because I don't know how to negotiate with sellers.
Should people learn how to bargain?
Not everyone needs to learn how to bargain. It depends on the culture and the situation. However, I think basic communication skills are useful everywhere.
What is the most expensive thing you've ever bought?
The most expensive thing I've ever bought is my iPhone. I didn't bargain because I bought it from a mall where the price was fixed. I regretted it a little because I bought accessory packages, like a phone case, a screen protector, and a power bank. Later, I found out they were much cheaper online.
Would you buy it online if you could do it again?
I would choose to buy it online because it is usually cheaper.
02 Lesson 2 · Checking Out 结账
LESSON 2

场景：收银台结账 — 支付方式、刷卡故障、找零与小票。

核心词汇 Vocabulary
check out
to pay and leave — 结账；付款离开
"Let's check out."我们去结账吧。
checkout
the counter where you pay — 收银台
"Where is the checkout?"收银台在哪里？
credit / debit card
信用卡 / 借记卡
"I'll pay by credit card."我用信用卡付款。
error
a mistake — 错误
"There seems to be an error."好像出了点问题。
password
密码
"It says my password is incorrect."它显示我的密码不正确。
change
the money returned — 找零
"Here is your change and your receipt."这是找零和小票。
receipt
收据 / 小票
"Do you have the receipt?"你带小票了吗？
常用表达 Useful Expressions
1
Where is the checkout?
收银台在哪里？
2
Will you pay by card or in cash?
你刷卡还是付现金？
3
I'll pay by credit card. / Can I pay by credit card?
我用信用卡付款。/ 我可以用信用卡付款吗？
4
There seems to be an error.
好像出了点问题。
5
It says my password is incorrect.
它显示我的密码不正确。
6
I completely forgot my password.
我完全忘了密码。
7
How about paying in cash?
用现金付款怎么样？
8
Here is your change and your receipt.
这是找零和小票。
9
The main reason is convenience.
主要原因是方便。
10
I don't need to carry cash.
我不需要带现金。
句子模式 Sentence Patterns

03 Lesson 3 · Return & Refund 退货与退款
LESSON 3

场景：退货退款 — 说明商品问题、与客服沟通、拿回退款。

核心词汇 Vocabulary
return
to give something back — 退货；归还
"I'd like to return this item."我想退这件商品。
refund
money given back — 退款
"I'd like to ask for a refund."我想申请退款。
turn off / on one's own
关机 / 自己、独自、自动地
"It keeps turning off on its own."它总是自己关机。
take care of
处理；照顾
"I'll take care of this and give you the refund."我来处理并给你退款。
defective / damaged
有瑕疵的 / 损坏的
"The item was defective."商品有瑕疵。
knockoff / substandard
假货 / 不合格的
"It turned out to be a knockoff."结果是个假货。
replacement
替换品；更换商品
"They sent me a replacement."他们给我寄来了一个替换品。
tax refund
退税
"I bought a bag abroad and applied for a tax refund."我在国外买了一个包并申请了退税。
常用表达 Useful Expressions
1
I'd like to return this item.
我想退这件商品。
2
I'd like to ask for a refund.
我想申请退款。
3
Is there something wrong with it?
这个东西有什么问题吗？
4
It keeps turning off on its own.
它总是自己关机。
5
It wouldn't heat up. There was something wrong with the power connection.
它无法加热。电源接口出了点问题。
6
Do you have the receipt with you? — I have it right here.
你带小票了吗？— 我带着，就在这里。
7
I'll take care of this and give you the refund.
我来处理并给你退款。
8
I sent a video to customer service. They sent me a replacement.
我给客服发了视频。他们给我寄来了一个替换品。
9
In most cases, they offer a replacement rather than a refund.
大多数情况下，他们提供换货，而不是退款。
10
The seller accepted my return quickly. I received the refund without any problems.
卖家很快就接受了退货。我顺利收到了退款。
11
They didn't fit me. They didn't look good on me.
它们不合身。它们穿在我身上不好看。
12
Return the product and ask for a refund.
退货并申请退款。
个人答案库 Personal Answer Bank
Have you ever returned something to a shop? Why?
Yes, I've returned clothes that I bought online, for example, on Taobao. Sometimes they didn't fit me or didn't look good on me, so I returned them. Many online stores offer a seven-day return policy without giving a reason, so the process was quite easy. I got my refund successfully.
Another example — a defective product
I also had a problem with a rice cooker I bought online. There was something wrong with the power connection, so it wouldn't heat up. I sent a video to customer service, and they sent me a replacement. In most cases, they offer a replacement rather than a refund.
Was the return process easy?
The seller accepted my return quickly, and I received the refund without any problems.
Do you support the seven-day return policy for online shopping?
Yes, I support it. I think this policy makes people feel less worried about buying things online, so they are more willing to make a purchase. At the same time, it encourages sellers to provide better products and better customer service.
Should physical stores also offer a seven-day return policy without giving a reason?
Personally, I don't think it's necessary for physical stores to offer a seven-day return policy without giving a reason. First, customers can check or try products before buying them, so they can make a better decision. Second, too many returns could increase operating costs and affect stores' profits. Some people might also take advantage of the policy.
04 Small Talk · Before Class 课前寒暄

Weather 天气

"It's still sunny here. The weather has been pretty nice lately."

这里还出太阳。最近天气相当不错。

"How's the weather where you are? Is it sunny there too?"

你那边天气怎么样？也出太阳吗？

Weekend 周末

"I had a really fun weekend! I went to a friend's place, and we had hot pot together. We also played badminton and a party game called Werewolf. It was a lot of fun!"

周末玩得很开心！去了朋友家吃火锅，还打了羽毛球、玩了狼人杀，特别好玩！

Useful Expressions 常用表达

go to a friend's place / house 去朋友家 have hot pot 吃火锅 play badminton 打羽毛球 play Werewolf 玩狼人杀 over the weekend 在周末期间 hang out with friends 和朋友一起玩、聚会 I'm not very good at Werewolf because I'm not good at lying. 我不太会玩狼人杀，因为我不擅长撒谎。 I'm a terrible liar! 我特别不会撒谎！（口语、带幽默）

"I love badminton. I play it regularly, about once a week. I usually play badminton with my friends, and I prefer playing for fun."

Ask the Teacher 反问老师

"How about you? How was your weekend?" / "What did you do over the weekend?"

"Do you usually hang out with your friends on weekends?" / "Have you ever played Werewolf before?"

Responding to Praise & Corrections 回应表扬与纠错

"Thank you! I'm still working on improving my speaking skills."

"Thank you! I feel more confident speaking English now, but I still need more practice."

"I see. Thank you for correcting me." / "That makes sense. I'll remember that."

06 Speaking Reminder 开口技巧

回答开放式问题时，使用：

Answer先回答 → Reason给理由 → Example举例子 → However补转折

Don't stop at a one-sentence answer. Add one detail, then ask the other person a question.

不要只答一句话就停。补充一个细节，然后反问对方一个问题。
