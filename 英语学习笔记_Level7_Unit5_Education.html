01 Educational Background｜教育背景

University / college、学位名称和学历表达

University vs. College

US: college 常指本科院校，也可泛指大学；常说 in college。University 通常含多个学院或学科。

UK: college 常指继续教育或职业教育机构；攻读学位通常说 go to university / at university。

US: Paul and his sister are both in college. · UK: Paul and his sister are both at university.

全称简称中文
Bachelor of Arts / ScienceBA / BS文学／理学学士
Master of Arts / ScienceMA / MS文学／理学硕士
Master of Business AdministrationMBA工商管理硕士
Doctor of Philosophy / MedicinePh.D. / M.D.哲学博士／医学博士
I got my BS in political science.
get a degree in + 专业

have a doctorate in + 专业

我的教育背景

I got my bachelor's degree in Automation from Zhengzhou University. Then I got my master's degree in Control Science and Engineering from Central South University.

学位来自某校用 from；专业名称在正式介绍中可首字母大写。

02 Professional Development｜职业发展与继续教育

区分职业能力提升和成人继续学习

professional development
完成基础教育后，为提升职业能力而积累技能和知识，如 coaching、on-the-job training。
continuing education
成年人继续参加课程和提升学历，如 night course、online certificate。
coach 指导on-the-job training 在岗培训night course 夜校课程certificate 证书qualification 学历／资历

Useful Examples

HR scheduled some on-the-job training so we can learn how to use the new software.

Ernie has a design degree, but now he's taking a night course in economics.

Maybe I'll get a certificate online.

03 Core Grammar｜核心语法

动名词、疑问词从句、-ever 与自然口语弱读

Preposition + V-ing｜介词后接动名词
think about doing
I've thought about doing some professional development.
be interested in doing
Jan's interested in coaching other employees.
plan on doing
He plans on getting his MBA.
look forward to doing
I look forward to taking a night course.

Statements with Question Words｜疑问词从句

疑问词从句使用陈述语序：I don't know where the school is.

错误：I don't know where is the school.

I wonder what the schedule is. · Bette knows who the instructor is.

Question Words + -ever
whatever 无论什么whenever 无论何时wherever 无论哪里whoever 无论谁however 无论以何种方式
听力提示：自然语流中的 you、and、to 等功能词经常弱读或缩短；look forward to 中的 to 是介词，后接名词或 V-ing。

05 Speaking Practice｜个人口语训练

教育经历、工作经验、德国博士动机与研究目标

本科与硕士专业的关系

Control Science and Engineering is closely related to my undergraduate major, Automation. It's a natural choice for Automation graduates who want to continue their studies at the master's level.

工作经历

After graduating, I worked as a software development engineer for four years. My main responsibilities included programming, system optimization, and problem-solving.

为什么继续攻读博士

I wanted to improve my professional skills, so I decided to continue my education. Robotics is becoming increasingly popular nowadays, and I think it's a promising field with great potential.

研究目标

I hope to develop new control methods and optimization algorithms to make robots more intelligent, efficient, and adaptable.

博士阶段的挑战

One of the biggest challenges is adapting to a new environment and improving my academic English. I also need to get used to a different academic culture and learn how to communicate with international researchers.

如何准备

I practice speaking English every day, read academic papers, and communicate with native speakers. I hope these activities will help me improve my communication skills and adapt to my new environment more quickly.

博士毕业目标

By the end of my Ph.D. studies, I hope to become an independent researcher. I also want to develop innovative methods in robot control and optimization and publish high-quality academic papers.
