01 Explaining a Problem｜描述问题
核心句型 + 个人问题词汇 + 实用搭配 + 课堂易错点

核心句型

The problem is that … — 问题是……

The trouble is that … — 麻烦在于……
EN
The problem is that people can't sleep well.

EN

EN

超重的
have a problem sleeping
有睡眠问题

描述问题时的实用搭配
He's addicted to coffee. 他对咖啡上瘾。 I have insomnia. 我有失眠问题。 I can't sleep well. 我睡不好。 They may lose a lot of money. 他们可能会损失很多钱。 They may have financial problems. 他们可能会遇到经济困难。 They have many bills to pay. 他们有很多账单要付。
课堂练习易错点： ① I'm overweight（不是 overweighting，overweight 是形容词不加 -ing）；② The problem is that I have insomnia（不是 I am insomnia，insomnia 是名词，用 have insomnia）；③ he/she/it 作一般现在时主语时行为动词加 -s：He drinks too much coffee；④ may 后面接动词原形：They may forget important tasks and miss deadlines。
02 Giving Advice｜给建议
常用句型 + 语法结构 + 课堂练习纠正

常用句型

You should … — 你应该……
You ought to … — 你应该……
You had better … / You'd better … — 你最好……（语气较强）
Make sure to … / Make sure not to … — 一定要…… / 一定不要……
Be sure to … — 务必…… · Don't … — 不要……
结构例句含义
should + 动词原形You should exercise more.你应该多锻炼。
ought to + 动词原形You ought to get enough sleep.你应该保证充足睡眠。
had better + 动词原形You had better quit smoking.你最好戒烟。
had better not + 动词原形You had better not drink coffee at night.你最好晚上不要喝咖啡。
Make sure to + 动词原形Make sure to get enough sleep every night.一定要保证每晚睡眠充足。
Make sure not to + 动词原形Make sure not to drink coffee at night.晚上一定不要喝咖啡。
课堂练习纠正： 不说 You should to exercise more；不说 You had better don't drink coffee at night（应说 You had better not drink…）；不说 You had better not to drink…；更自然地说法：Make sure to get enough sleep（睡眠充足，不说 sleep enough time）。

05 Offering Solutions｜提出解决方案
核心句型 + 例句 + 课堂表达

核心句型

One solution could be to … — 一个解决办法可以是……
Another possibility would be to … — 另一个可能的办法是……
I strongly suggest that we … — 我强烈建议我们……
The only solution is to … — 唯一的解决办法是……
The only way to solve this problem is to … — 解决这个问题的唯一办法是……
EN
One solution could be to have a team meeting and improve communication.
一个解决办法是召开团队会议并改善沟通。
EN
Another possibility would be to hire a new manager.
另一个可能的办法是聘请一位新经理。
EN
I strongly suggest that we deal with this problem immediately.
我强烈建议我们立即处理这个问题。
EN
One solution could be to reduce costs.
一个解决办法可以是降低成本。
注意： team meeting 在职场语境中很自然；Another possibility would be to … 是课件重点句型（could 也可以表达可能性，但练习时先熟悉课件句型）。

08 课后作业 Part 2｜Sympathizing with Someone
表达关心、理解和安慰 + 情景对话

表达关心、理解和安慰

I'm sorry to hear that. — 听到这个我很难过。
That sounds difficult. — 听起来确实不容易。
That must be difficult. — 那一定很不容易。
I hope everything will be all right. — 希望一切都会好起来。
I hope everything gets better soon. — 希望一切很快好起来。
I hope things get better soon. — 希望情况很快好转。
表达区别： That sounds difficult — 听起来很不容易，回应对方描述的情况；That must be difficult — 那一定很不容易，更直接地表达理解。安慰后可提出建议：You should … / You should go to bed earlier. / Stop playing computer games at night.
情景对话
情景 A：工作压力
Friend: I've been very stressed at work lately. I feel tired every day.
You: I'm sorry to hear that. That sounds difficult. I hope everything will be all right.
情景 B：经济困难
Friend: I've been having financial problems. I don't have enough money to pay all my bills.
You: I'm sorry to hear that. That must be difficult. I hope everything gets better soon.
情景 C：沉迷电脑游戏、失眠
Friend: I play computer games every night, and now I have insomnia. I feel tired all day.
You: I'm sorry to hear that. You should go to bed earlier and stop playing computer games at night.
09 今日重点易错点汇总
8 个最容易出错的地方
I'm overweight.overweight 是形容词（不是 I'm overweighting.）
I have insomnia.insomnia 是名词（不是 I am insomnia.）
You should go to bed earlier.should + 动词原形
You had better not drink coffee at night.had better not + 动词原形
They may forget important tasks and miss deadlines.may + 动词原形
He drinks coffee. / It faces fierce competition.第三人称单数一般现在时加 -s
Make sure to get enough sleep every night.get enough sleep（不说 sleep enough time）
go to bed earlier · stop playing …常用搭配：affect my health / pay my bills / face fierce competition / miss deadlines
