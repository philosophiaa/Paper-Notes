02 Describing a TV Program｜描述电视节目

-ed / -ing 形容词与非正式口语省略

事物令人……人的感受对比例句
boringboredThe show is boring. / I am bored.
confusingconfusedThe plot is confusing. / I’m confused.
excitingexcitedThe movie is exciting. / Dan is excited.
interestinginterestedThe news is interesting. / I’m interested.
amazingamazedThe movie is amazing. / I’m amazed.

Dropping Subjects and Verbs｜口语省略

非正式对话中，可省略上下文已知的主语和动词：

Did you say “City Wolf”? → “City Wolf”?

It’s about a police detective in Seattle. → A police detective in Seattle.

The plot is fascinating. → Fascinating plot.

注意：这种省略适合非正式口语，正式写作应使用完整句子。

04 Writing About TV｜电视观点写作

because / because of 与观点、理由、建议结构

结构例句
because + 完整句子I don’t watch it because it’s violent.
because of + 名词短语The show wasn’t broadcast because of the violence.
Giving an Opinion｜表达观点
I was surprised at …In my opinion, …I feel strongly that …For example, …In fact, …

Opinion → Support → Suggestion

观点：In my opinion, TV can help educate children.

支持：For example, TV can show children countries and places they may never visit.

建议：We need to help our children choose the right shows and watch TV with them.

05 Speaking Practice｜个人口语训练

Lesson 1–2 实际对话的优化答案与重点纠错

观看习惯

I generally watch reality shows and sometimes watch dramas. I usually watch TV in the evening, but sometimes I watch it all day on weekends.

不喜欢的节目

I’m not crazy about game shows. I think they’re silly.

泛指节目类型使用复数 game shows，并用 they / they’re。

电视是否仍然重要

Television is still important because it has a bigger screen. It can provide news and useful information, such as educational programs for kids. However, smartphones are more convenient.

真人秀的价值

I prefer reality shows because they show real people and real situations. People can learn about different lifestyles and see how others live. However, we shouldn’t believe everything we see on TV.

理想中的 AI 电视剧

I’d like to create a TV drama generated by AI. It could have interesting stories and different endings based on the audience’s choices.

电视的优缺点

Television has more advantages than disadvantages. It brings us entertainment, educational programs, and useful information. However, watching too much TV may waste our time and affect our health.

最喜欢的节目 See You Again

I’m really into reality shows, such as “See You Again.” There are four pairs of former friends who drifted apart for different reasons. The program follows their journey as they have heart-to-heart conversations and try to resolve past conflicts.

I can learn about communication, relationships, and friendship from the show.

节目能否改善关系

We can understand our friends better and learn to communicate openly and resolve misunderstandings. It also helps us value friendship more.
