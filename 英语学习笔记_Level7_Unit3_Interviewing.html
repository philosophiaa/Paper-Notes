01 Responding to a Job Advertisement｜回应招聘广告

职位信息、Resume 与 CV、求职邮件结构

Job Advertisement｜招聘广告组成
job title 职位名称job description 职位描述duties 工作职责education / experience 教育／经验salary and benefits 薪水和福利
Manage office operations and supervise a staff of 20.
管理办公室运营并监督 20 名员工。
Maintain office staff by hiring and training employees.
通过招聘和培训维持人员配置。
Assign and monitor tasks among office staff.
分配并跟进办公室员工的任务。
Resume vs. CV
文件主要用途
Resume美式英语中用于一般求职，简要概述工作经历与资历。
CV美式英语中更全面，主要用于学术或科研申请；英式英语中是更通用的简历称呼。
Application Email｜求职邮件结构
  1. 称呼：To whom it may concern:
  2. 目的：I am writing in response to your online ad for an office manager.
  3. 兴趣：I am very interested in the position.
  4. 附件：I have attached my resume.
  5. 时间：I am available for an interview anytime in the next few weeks.
  6. 结尾：I look forward to hearing from you.
语法：look forward to + 名词 / V-ing。正确：I look forward to hearing from you。

Scheduling an Interview｜预约对话

A: I’m calling to schedule an interview. When would you like to come in?

B: How about Thursday afternoon?

A: I can see you Thursday at 3. Is that good for you?

B: Yes, that works for me.

A: I’ve got you scheduled for Thursday afternoon at 3.

B: Yes, that’s correct. Thank you very much.

03 Interviewing for a Job｜参加求职面试

面试词汇、过去完成时、礼仪和常见问题

strengths 优势quick learner 学习能力强references 推荐人applicants 申请者hardworking 勤奋的motivated 积极主动的

Past Perfect｜过去完成时

had + past participle，说明两个过去动作中先发生的动作。

When he arrived at the interview, Mr. Jones had gone.

I’d sent my resume before I went to the interview.

有 before / after 且先后关系清楚时，也可使用一般过去时。

04 Ending an Interview｜结束面试

间接疑问句、礼貌获取信息与专业告别

Indirect Questions｜间接疑问句

What is the salary? → Can you tell me what the salary is?

Could you tell me what the office environment is like?

Could you explain how decisions are made?

Can you tell me who the team members are?

Polite Questions｜礼貌提问
Could you tell me more about the benefits package?
您能介绍一下福利待遇吗？
I was wondering about the salary range.
我想了解一下薪资范围。
Would you mind telling me about your overtime policy?
您介意介绍一下加班政策吗？
语法：Would you mind + V-ing；间接疑问句使用“疑问词 + 主语 + 谓语”的陈述句语序。
Closing｜专业结束
It was very nice to meet you.
很高兴见到您。
Thank you for the opportunity to interview with your company.
感谢贵公司的面试机会。
Please let me know if there’s any other information I can provide.
如需其他信息，请告诉我。
I look forward to hearing from you soon.
期待尽快收到您的回复。

05 Mock Interview｜个人模拟面试

补充课堂练习，与课件原文分开整理

Work Experience｜工作经历

Q: Could you tell me a little more about your work experience?

I’ve been working as a software development engineer at a technology company for four years. I’m good at coding, data analysis, and optimization. I’m also very interested in robotics and artificial intelligence.

用 technology company；coding 拼写；data analysis and optimization 保持并列结构一致。

Strengths｜优势

One of my biggest strengths is my problem-solving ability. I’m also a quick learner.

结构：One of my + 最高级 + 名词复数。

Weaknesses｜弱点与改进

One of my weaknesses is that I sometimes lack confidence when communicating in English. I’m improving my English and practicing speaking every day. This will help me communicate more effectively in an international environment.

Career Interests｜职业兴趣

I’ve always been interested in robotics, especially robot control and optimization. I’m particularly interested in combining traditional control methods with AI to develop smarter and more reliable robots.

Career Goals｜五年目标

In five years, I hope to become an independent researcher. By then, I hope to have developed my research skills and gained experience working on challenging robotics projects. Ultimately, I want to contribute to the development of intelligent robots.

hope to have + 过去分词：希望到未来某个时间点已经完成。

Teamwork｜团队合作

In my previous job, I worked with a team on a software development project. We faced technical problems, and my role was to analyze them and help find solutions. I worked closely with my colleagues, and together we completed the project successfully.

Handling Pressure｜应对压力

I try to stay calm and focus on the most important things. I break big tasks into smaller ones and deal with them step by step.

Benefits & Closing｜福利与结束

Could you tell me more about the benefits?

No, that’s all for now. Thank you very much for your time. It was nice talking with you. I look forward to hearing from you.

06 Teacher’s Feedback｜老师课堂反馈

教育背景、实习经历、职位名称和工作经验匹配度的表达纠错

Good Things｜表现良好的词句
I have similar work experience.I believe I’m good at communicating.communication 沟通bachelor’s degree 学士学位I had worked 我此前工作过
用法提示：截图中的 I’m good with communication 可以理解，但更自然的说法是 I’m good at communicating 或 I have good communication skills。
Practice｜重点纠错
原句正确表达要点
❌ I have the bachelor of Commerce.✅ I have a bachelor’s degree in Commerce.学历用 a bachelor’s degree in + 专业。
❌ I had worked as a inter between for a year.✅ I worked as an intern for a year.intern 前用 an；描述已结束的一年经历用一般过去时。
❌ I worked as an operation support at EC Engineering.✅ I worked in operations support at EC Engineering.
✅ I worked as an operations support specialist at EC Engineering.		operations support 是工作领域；如用 work as，后面最好接明确职位名称。
To Say / To Do｜继续练习
I worked as a data analyst for five years.
我做了五年数据分析师。
I have good communication skills.
我具备良好的沟通能力。skills 是复数，不加 a。
My work experience is a good fit for the position I’m applying for.
我的工作经验很符合我申请的职位。
My work experience matches the requirements of this position.
我的工作经验符合该职位的要求。
本次重点：a bachelor’s degree in … · work as an intern · work in operations support · good communication skills · be a good fit for a position。
