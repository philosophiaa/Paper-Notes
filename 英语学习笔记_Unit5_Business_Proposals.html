01 Describing a Business Situation｜描述商务现状

掌握商业数据词汇，清楚表达原因、结果、增长和下降

Key Vocabulary｜核心词汇
revenue 营业收入profits 利润market share 市场份额region 地区purchase 购买／收购strength 优势sales 销售额efficiency 效率competition 竞争competitor 竞争对手
Useful Examples｜实用例句
Company revenue has increased.
公司营业收入增长了。
Europe is a very important region for us.
欧洲对我们来说是一个非常重要的地区。
Do we have enough money for the purchase?
我们有足够的钱进行这项收购吗？
Our market share is only 5% in that country.
我们在那个国家的市场份额只有 5%。
The biggest strength we have is our technology.
我们最大的优势是技术。
What can we do to increase sales?
我们可以做些什么来提高销售额？
Cause and Effect｜原因与结果
表达用法例句
because of / due to后接名词或名词短语，说明原因Due to the purchase of Sunset Computers, our market share has increased.
as a result引出前述原因造成的结果Sales in Europe have decreased. As a result, revenue there fell by 8%.
consequently较正式地表达“因此”Our costs have risen. Consequently, our profits are going to decrease.
标点提示：because of / due to 引导的原因短语位于句首时，后面通常加逗号；位于主句后时一般不加。
Increases and Decreases｜增长与下降

Present Perfect｜现在完成时

结构：have / has + past participle，用于描述持续到现在的变化。

Our market share has increased to 23 percent.

Profits have decreased by 15 percent.

Sales have been flat.

Past Simple｜一般过去时

用于描述过去某个明确时间段的数据。

Revenue fell by 8% last year.

Our market share rose to 35% last quarter.

to 与 by：increase / decrease / rise / fall to + 最终数值；by + 变化幅度。例：increased to 23%（增长到 23%）；decreased by 15%（下降了 15%）。
nearly 将近slightly 略微significantly 显著地remain flat 保持不变
Model Answer｜商务现状描述范例

The purpose of today’s meeting is to talk about our competition. Their increasing market share is a big problem for us. Overall, our revenue and profits are down. We have to find ways to improve our sales. We cannot delay.

One thing we are thinking about is the purchase of one of our smaller competitors. They have a lot of strength in the South American region. We are also looking at several projects to improve efficiency.

02 Discussing Possible Solutions｜讨论可能的解决方案

表达观点、回应观点，并在讨论中请求重复、澄清和确认

Think & Believe｜表达观点
I think (that) Joan did a good job.
我认为 Joan 做得很好。that 可以省略。
Simon believes (that) we can save half a million dollars.
Simon 相信我们可以节省 50 万美元。
Yes, I think so.
是的，我也这么认为。so 代替前面的从句。
No, I don’t think so.
不，我不这么认为。
In my opinion, …It’s my feeling that …I think …I believe …
Repetition, Clarification & Confirmation｜重复、澄清与确认
目的实用表达
请求重复I’m sorry, could you repeat that? / I didn’t catch that. / Would you mind repeating that?
请求澄清Do you mean that you think it’s a good idea? / Are you saying we should stop the project?
请求确认So, just to confirm, you believe that it’s too expensive? / Half a million?
Verb to Noun｜动词变名词
discuss → discussion 讨论suggest → suggestion 建议hire → hiring 招聘save → savings 节省／储蓄propose → proposal 提案think → thought 想法lay off → layoff 裁员
注意：不存在适用于所有单词的固定转换规则。有些名词变化较大，有些与动词形式相同，需要分别记忆。
Dialogue｜讨论削减成本

Sarah: In my opinion, we need to cut costs.

Paul: Yes, but how?

Sarah: I think that we could save half a million dollars with layoffs.

Paul: No, I don’t believe that’s the best idea. It would hurt employee morale.

Sarah: So, what’s your idea?

Paul: It’s my feeling that a hiring freeze is the best way.

Sarah: Maybe, but it will take longer to save money.

cut costs 削减成本layoffs 裁员employee morale 员工士气hiring freeze 冻结招聘

03 Making a Proposal｜提出商务提案

使用特殊不定式结构以及 propose / suggest 正式提出方案

Special Infinitive Phrases｜特殊不定式结构
This meeting is an opportunity to give you some answers.
名词 + to do：这次会议是一个向你们提供答案的机会。
We’re not looking at layoffs to save money.
我们并不考虑通过裁员来省钱。
The purpose of today’s meeting is to give you a company update.
名词短语 + be + to do：今天会议的目的是介绍公司最新情况。
Propose & Suggest｜正式提出建议

核心结构

I propose (that) we delay the database project.

I suggest (that) we hire a new project manager.

I suggest that we not have any layoffs.

He proposes that the company not give pay increases this year.

语法重点：propose / suggest 后的 that 可以省略；that 从句使用动词原形，不因主语是 he / she / the company 而加 -s。否定形式为 not + 动词原形。
Dialogue｜节省资金的方案

Ron: OK, so what are your ideas for saving money?

Linda: I have two proposals. First, I propose that we delay the database project for six months.

Ron: How much will that save us?

Linda: Half a million dollars.

Ron: That’s great! And your second idea?

Linda: I suggest that the company not lay off any staff, and that we have a hiring freeze. In other words, as staff leaves, we don’t replace them.

proposal 提案delay the project 推迟项目lay off staff 裁员replace staff 补充／替换员工

04 Writing a Short Business Proposal｜撰写简短商务提案

组织执行摘要，比较正式与非正式语言，分析优缺点并完成结论

Executive Summary｜执行摘要结构
步骤功能示例
1. Purpose说明提案目的The purpose of this proposal is to find a new location for our company offices.
2. Problem总结需要解决的问题Because of falling market share, we have to find a way to generate more sales.
3. Actions taken描述已经采取的行动We have reviewed a list of options to find the best solution.
4. Solution概述推荐方案We are proposing a hiring freeze as the best way to cut expenses.
5. Rejected alternatives解释为何拒绝其他方案We are rejecting layoffs due to the negative effects on staff.
Formal vs. Informal｜正式与非正式表达
功能InformalFormal
说明目的I think we should try to spend less money.The purpose of this proposal is to outline a plan to cut expenses.
总结问题I think we should save more because things are getting more expensive, and sales are poor.Because of increasing expenses and decreasing sales, the company has identified the need to make savings.
已采取行动I’ve looked at a few options.We have reviewed a list of options to find the best solution.
提出方案I think we should have a hiring freeze.We are proposing a hiring freeze as the best way to cut expenses.
拒绝方案I don’t think we should lay off anyone.We are rejecting layoffs due to the negative effects they would have on staff.
Advantages & Disadvantages｜优缺点
One advantage is lower costs.
一个优点是成本较低。
Another pro is that revenues will increase.
另一个优点是收入会增加。
One con, however, is that we’ll need to use staff more efficiently.
然而，一个缺点是需要更有效地安排员工。
A further disadvantage is that projects will not be finished so quickly.
另一个缺点是项目完成速度会变慢。
pros and cons 优缺点a big advantage 重要优点an obvious advantage 明显优点a further disadvantage 另一项缺点
Concluding a Proposal｜总结提案
In conclusion, a hiring freeze is the best choice for the company right now.
总结解决方案。
The advantages are obvious: Costs would be cut by 50%.
重申方案优点。
We suggest that we review this proposal at a meeting next week.
建议下一步行动。
Please contact me with any questions or concerns.
邀请对方提出问题或顾虑。
Sample Proposal｜商务提案范例

The purpose of this proposal is to find a solution to our sales problems.

Sales have been falling due to more customers buying our competitors’ products.

We’ve reviewed a number of options, and we’ve decided a new product is the best option.

We’ve rejected the idea of upgrading our current products because they’re too old.

Can you let me know what you think before Friday?

Useful Email Expressions｜商务邮件常用表达
The purpose of this email is to begin discussion about a new office location.
这封邮件的目的是开始讨论新的办公室地点。
Our office space is too small due to the increase in employees.
由于员工人数增加，我们的办公空间太小了。
We are rejecting the solution offered by the operations manager.
我们不采用运营经理提出的方案。
We are going to review other proposals at tomorrow’s meeting.
我们将在明天的会议上审查其他提案。
We may have to lay off some employees because of budget cuts.
由于预算削减，我们可能不得不裁掉一些员工。
We have to cut the budget.
我们必须削减预算。

Practice 1｜描述数据变化
需要修改推荐表达要点
Our profit have increased significantly … and our profit have decrease …Our profit increased significantly from 2018 to 2020 and decreased from 2021 to 2022.profit 作单数时用 has；但明确的过去时间段更自然地使用一般过去时。
There is a significant increase of demand.There is a significant increase in demand.表示某方面的增长用 an increase in …
The graph shows that our revenue is decreasing.The graph shows that our revenue is decreasing.正确：图表显示营业收入正在下降。
There is a sharp decrease with our sales.There is a sharp decrease in our sales.表示销售额下降用 a decrease in sales。
Practice 2｜提出观点与顾虑
I think that would be a good solution / suggestion / point / idea.
我认为那会是一个不错的解决方案／建议／观点／想法。可数名词前需要 a。
I have a concern about this.
我对此有一个顾虑。比 But I have a problem 更具体、专业。
We might have a problem hiring more people because it would increase our costs.
招聘更多员工可能会带来问题，因为这会增加成本。
Practice 3｜Suggest 与 Propose
需要修改推荐表达要点
We suggesting that hiring freeze.We suggest a hiring freeze.
We suggest that the company implement a hiring freeze.		suggest 不能直接写成 we suggesting；that 从句使用动词原形。
We proposing that we can reduce in-person meeting and impose more online meeting.We propose reducing in-person meetings and holding more online meetings.用 propose doing 更简洁；meeting 应使用复数；hold a meeting 比 impose a meeting 自然。
We shouldn’t have lay off because it should lower morale.We shouldn’t lay off staff because it would lower morale.shouldn’t 后接动词原形；staff 通常是集合名词，不写 staffs。
To Say / To Do｜继续练习
I suggest that we find a cheaper supplier.
我建议我们找一家更便宜的供应商。
I think it’s time for the company to change its production equipment.
我认为公司该更换生产设备了。
I propose that we lay off some staff.
我提议裁掉一些员工。staff 不需要加 -s。
本次重点：an increase / decrease in · suggest / propose + 动词原形 · should + 动词原形 · lay off staff · hold online meetings。
