01 Welcoming Business Guests｜欢迎商务访客

欢迎、招待和介绍来宾时的自然表达

I’m so glad you could come.
很高兴你能来。
It’s great to see you in person.
很高兴见到你本人。
Can I get you something to drink?
要给你拿点喝的吗？
Do you know Kendra?
你认识 Kendra 吗？
You’ve met Brandon, haven’t you?
你见过 Brandon 了，对吧？
I’d like to introduce you to Mr. Klein.
我想把你介绍给 Klein 先生。
in person 当面、亲自get someone a drink 给某人拿饮料introduce A to B 把 A 介绍给 B

02 Making Small Talk｜寒暄

在正式谈工作前，用天气、活动、工作、家乡、旅行与兴趣让对话自然展开

Wow, it’s so hot outside!
外面真热！
This event is very well-planned.
这次活动安排得很好。
So, what do you do for a living?
那你是做什么工作的？
Are you from around here?
你是附近的人吗？
Have you always lived in the area?
你一直住在这个地区吗？
Are you taking any time off this summer?
今年夏天你会休假吗？
Self-introduction｜课堂简短版

Hi, I’m [Your Name]. I live in Shenzhen, close to Hong Kong and the sea.

I work for a technology company.

In my free time, I enjoy playing badminton, going to the gym, and traveling.

I’ve been to Thailand, Italy, and Spain. I love beautiful natural scenery and local food. My favorite dishes are Tom Yum soup in Thailand, pasta and pizza in Italy, and grilled meat and Spanish ham in Spain.

Nice to meet you!

语法提示： enjoy 后接动词 -ing：enjoy playing / going / traveling。I’ve been to … 使用现在完成时，表示曾经去过某地。
外教可能追问
What do you do for a living?What do you usually do in your free time?How often do you play badminton?Which country did you like the most?What was your favorite food in Italy?Do you prefer beaches or mountains?

04 Conversation Management｜对话管理

争取思考时间、表示倾听，以及处理没听清和没理解

场景实用表达中文提示
需要思考Hmm… / Let’s see…嗯…… / 让我想想……
认真倾听Uh-huh. / Right. / Interesting.嗯嗯。/ 对。/ 有意思。
没听清楚Could you repeat that? / Could you say that again, please?请对方重复
没有理解What do you mean? / I’m not sure I understand.听到了，但不理解意思
注意区分： 没听清用 Could you repeat that?；听到了但没理解用 I’m not sure I understand.

06 Interrupting Politely｜礼貌地打断

先致歉或确认对方是否正忙

Sorry to disturb you.
不好意思打扰你。
Are you in the middle of something?
你现在正在忙吗？

07 Grammar｜语法重点

反意疑问句与常见否定前缀

Negative Tag Questions｜肯定陈述 + 否定尾句

You work in marketing, don’t you?

You are the new sales manager, aren’t you?

You’ve met Brandon, haven’t you?

Affirmative Tag Questions｜否定陈述 + 肯定尾句

You don’t remember his name, do you?

She didn’t like the food, did she?

Tom isn’t the CEO, is he?

记忆方法： 前面肯定，后面否定；前面否定，后面肯定；前后动词和时态保持一致。
Negative Prefixes｜否定前缀
un- unhappy · unusualim- impossible · impolitein- inappropriate · inefficient

08 Small Talk 快速复习

名字 → 居住地 → 工作 → 兴趣爱好 → 旅游经历 → 喜欢的美食

优先记住

I live in Shenzhen, close to Hong Kong and the sea.

I work for a technology company.

In my free time, I enjoy playing badminton and going to the gym.

I’ve been to Thailand, Italy, and Spain.

I love beautiful natural scenery and local food.

口语建议： 自我介绍只是 Small Talk 的开场，不需要一次背完整段落。根据对方的追问，选择工作、兴趣、旅行或美食中的一个话题继续交流。
