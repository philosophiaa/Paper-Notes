Self-introduction｜课堂简短版

Hi, I’m [Your Name]. I live in Shenzhen, close to Hong Kong and the sea.

I work for a technology company.

In my free time, I enjoy playing badminton, going to the gym, and traveling.

I’ve been to Thailand, Italy, and Spain. I love beautiful natural scenery and local food. My favorite dishes are Tom Yum soup in Thailand, pasta and pizza in Italy, and grilled meat and Spanish ham in Spain.

Nice to meet you!

语法提示： enjoy 后接动词 -ing：enjoy playing / going / traveling。I’ve been to … 使用现在完成时，表示曾经去过某地。

04 Conversation Management｜对话管理

争取思考时间、表示倾听，以及处理没听清和没理解

场景实用表达中文提示
需要思考Hmm… / Let’s see…嗯…… / 让我想想……
认真倾听Uh-huh. / Right. / Interesting.嗯嗯。/ 对。/ 有意思。
没听清楚Could you repeat that? / Could you say that again, please?请对方重复
没有理解What do you mean? / I’m not sure I understand.听到了，但不理解意思
注意区分： 没听清用 Could you repeat that?；听到了但没理解用 I’m not sure I understand.

07 Grammar｜语法重点

反意疑问句与常见否定前缀

Negative Tag Questions｜肯定陈述 + 否定尾句

You work in marketing, don’t you?

You are the new sales manager, aren’t you?

You’ve met Brandon, haven’t you?

Affirmative Tag Questions｜否定陈述 + 肯定尾句

You don’t remember his name, do you?

She didn’t like the food, did she?

Tom isn’t the CEO, is he?

记忆方法： 前面肯定，后面否定；前面否定，后面肯定；前后动词和时态保持一致。
Negative Prefixes｜否定前缀
un- unhappy · unusualim- impossible · impolitein- inappropriate · inefficient

08 Summarizing a Networking Event｜总结商务社交活动

a successful event 一次成功的活动a networking event 商务交流活动potential business partners 潜在商业伙伴company representatives 公司代表exchange business cards 交换名片exchange ideas 交流想法a valuable experience 一次宝贵的经历business opportunities 商业机会

Expressing Opinions｜表达观点
I think… 我认为……In my opinion… 在我看来……I believe… 我相信……I guess… 我想……It seems that… 看起来……Overall… 总体来说……

Present Perfect｜现在完成时

结构：Subject + have / has + past participle

用途：描述过去发生、但与现在有关的经历或情况。

We have met several potential business partners.

Some participants have shown interest in our products.

We have established new business relationships.

常见错误： Some of them have showed interest. → Some of them have shown interest. 常见不规则变化：show–showed–shown；meet–met–met；make–made–made；write–wrote–written；give–gave–given；take–took–taken。

10 Follow-up Email｜商务跟进邮件

用“目的—活动概况—成果—下一步—结尾”的结构汇报社交活动

Subject: Summary of the Networking Event

Hi Team,

I’d like to share a brief summary of the networking event.

Overall, it was a very successful event. I met several potential business partners and exchanged business cards with representatives from different companies.

We also discussed our communication equipment and technical support services. Some participants showed interest in learning more about our products.

I believe this event was a valuable opportunity to develop new business relationships.

I’ll follow up with these contacts and keep you updated.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

邮件结构： 1. Opening：说明目的　2. Event Summary：概述活动　3. Key Results：说明商务机会　4. Next Steps：列出跟进计划　5. Closing：专业结尾

✍ Practice｜重点纠错
原句修改提示正确表达
❌ She had shown excited our product.She had shown ______ our product.✅ She had shown excitement to our product.
❌ Who are form Fintech Solutions?注意主谓一致与拼写：are → is；form → from✅ Who is from Fintech Solutions?
❌ Should I call to her?Should I ____ her?✅ Should I call her?
更自然的表达： She showed interest in our product. / She was excited about our product. 注意 excited 是形容词，excitement 是名词。
