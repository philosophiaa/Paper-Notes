01 Talking About the Environment｜讨论环境

环境、能源、环保行动与第一条件句

Key Vocabulary｜核心词汇
coal 煤炭natural gas 天然气gasoline 汽油pollution 污染electric car 电动汽车charging station 充电站air quality 空气质量clean energy 清洁能源public transportation 公共交通
Useful Expressions｜重点表达
reduce air pollution
减少空气污染
improve air quality
改善空气质量
charge an electric car
给电动汽车充电
use clean energy / save energy
使用清洁能源／节约能源

First Conditional｜第一条件句

If + 一般现在时, 主语 + will + 动词原形.

If more people use electric cars, air pollution will decrease.

If the city changes to natural gas, air quality will improve.

注意：If 从句不用 will。正确：If more people use electric cars …；错误：If more people will use …
Changes Over Time｜描述变化
结构用途例句
am / is / are + V-ing正在发生的变化Opinions about electric cars are changing.
have / has been + V-ing过去开始并持续到现在Air quality has been improving for several years.
get + adjective逐渐变得The environment is getting better.
发音：-ion 结尾的单词重音通常在倒数第二个音节，如 pollution、information、education、transportation、tradition。

02 Talking About Future Technology｜未来科技

预测机器人与医疗技术，并表达惊讶、怀疑和态度

Vocabulary｜核心词汇
the elderly 老年人cancer cells 癌细胞medical scanner 医疗扫描仪genetic information 遗传信息creativity 创造力medical technology 医疗技术
Predicting the Future｜预测未来
Robots will take care of the elderly.
will + 动词原形：未来预测。
Doctors are going to have new medical scanners.
be going to + 动词原形：未来预测。
I believe (that) technology will change our lives.
that 通常可以省略。
Robots may / might / could become smarter.
may、might、could 表达可能性。

Future Ability｜未来能力

will be able to + 动词原形

Robots will be able to take care of the elderly.

Doctors will be able to detect cancer earlier.

不能说：will able to do。

Reactions & Attitudes｜反应与态度
功能表达
惊讶／赞叹That’s surprising! · That’s amazing! · Shocking! · It’s unbelievable!
怀疑I doubt it. · I find that hard to believe. · That’ll never happen.
乐观I’m optimistic. · I’m optimistic overall.
悲观／不确定I’m pessimistic. · I’m not sure. · I really don’t know what will happen.

Speaking Model｜口语范例

I think robots will do more work in the future. They will be able to take care of the elderly and children, do housework, and help people complete their daily tasks.

I don’t think robots can completely replace humans. Robots are tools that help us work more efficiently, but they can’t replace human creativity.

Medical technology will become more advanced. Doctors will be able to detect cancer earlier and save more lives.

03 Discussing Business Changes｜商业变化

创新、远程办公、目的表达与数量比较

realistic 现实的demonstrate 演示teleconference 远程会议effective 有效的efficient 高效的market share 市场份额innovation 创新innovative 创新的

First Conditional with Unless

Unless = If … not，意为“除非／如果不”。

Unless the team can follow the schedule, development will fall behind.

Unless we innovate constantly, we won’t stay competitive.

注意：Unless 已经包含否定含义，通常不再加 not。Unless we innovate …，不能写 Unless we don’t innovate …
Expressing Purpose｜表达目的
We need more time to brainstorm.
to + 动词原形
We invest in R&D in order to develop competitive products.
in order to + 动词原形，更正式。
We changed the schedule so that we have time for innovation.
so that + 完整句子
More, Fewer & Less｜数量表达
词搭配例句
more可数与不可数名词more employees / more work
fewer可数名词复数fewer people / fewer hours
less不可数名词less time / less gasoline / less work

06 Writing Practice｜未来主题写作

题目：What do you believe will happen in the future? Are you optimistic or pessimistic?（60–100 words）

Version 1 · Optimistic｜乐观型

I believe life in the future will be better and more convenient. Technology will become more advanced, and robots will help people with daily tasks. Medical services will also improve, and doctors will be able to detect diseases earlier. In addition, more people will use clean energy to reduce pollution. However, people may become too dependent on technology. Overall, I am optimistic about the future because technology will make our lives easier and more colorful.

我相信未来生活会更好、更方便。科技和医疗将进步，清洁能源会减少污染，但人们也可能过度依赖科技。

Version 2 · Balanced｜平衡型

I think the future will be very different from today. Technology will become more advanced, and people will have better healthcare and more convenient transportation. Robots will be able to do more work and help people in their daily lives. However, some people may lose their jobs because of technology. People may also depend too much on robots. I am optimistic overall because I believe technology will bring more opportunities and improve our lives.

Version 3 · Technology and Robots｜科技与机器人

I believe robots and artificial intelligence will play an important role in the future. Robots will be able to take care of the elderly, do housework and help people at work. Medical technology will also become more advanced and help save more lives. However, I don’t think robots will completely replace humans because some jobs require human creativity and skills. I am optimistic about the future, but I think we should use technology wisely.

Writing Structure｜写作结构

  1. 开头：I believe … / I think …，说明总体看法。
  2. 中间：选择科技、机器人、医疗、交通或环保中的 2–3 个方面。
  3. 转折：用 However 补充潜在问题。
  4. 结尾：Overall, I am optimistic / pessimistic …，明确态度。
